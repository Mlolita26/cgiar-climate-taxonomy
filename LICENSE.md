## Why attribution and not public domain

Many definitions in this vocabulary are drawn from, and cite, published sources (the IPCC AR6
glossary, AGROVOC, FAO, WMO, UNFCCC). Attribution keeps that chain of credit visible when the
vocabulary is reused. Definitions quoted from those sources remain subject to their own terms;
the vocabulary cites the source of every definition.
