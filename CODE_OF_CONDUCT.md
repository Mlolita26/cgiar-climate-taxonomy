# Code of conduct

This vocabulary is maintained in the open, and people from many institutions, disciplines and
countries take part in it. This code applies to everyone who takes part: in issues, discussions,
pull requests, review sessions and any other space connected with the taxonomy.

## Our commitment

We want taking part to be a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever
their age, body, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be respectful of differing views and experience. Disagreements about a term are normal; argue
  from definitions, sources and evidence of use.
- Give and accept constructive criticism. Explain a decision, and accept that a proposal may be
  refused for a stated reason.
- Credit the work of others, including the sources that definitions draw on.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use the vocabulary.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a postal or email address, without their
  explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to one of the custodians, Andreea Nowak or Lolita Muller (Alliance of
Bioversity International and CIAT). If you do not have their contact details, open an issue that
only asks to be contacted, without details, and a custodian will reach you. Reports are handled in
confidence.

## What the custodians do

The custodians review every report, act fairly, and explain their decision to those involved. They
may remove or edit comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may, for repeated
or serious breaches, exclude a person from taking part, temporarily or permanently.
