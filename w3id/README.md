# CGIAR

Permanent identifiers for vocabularies maintained in CGIAR, the global research partnership for a
food-secure future (https://www.cgiar.org).

## climate-taxonomy

`https://w3id.org/cgiar/climate-taxonomy/` identifies the CGIAR Climate Adaptation and Mitigation
Taxonomy, a SKOS vocabulary of concepts for describing climate adaptation and mitigation in
agricultural research results. Each concept is identified by
`https://w3id.org/cgiar/climate-taxonomy/<ID>`, for example `.../CRK_102`.

- Home page: https://mlolita26.github.io/cgiar-climate-taxonomy/
- Repository: https://github.com/Mlolita26/cgiar-climate-taxonomy
