CGIAR Climate Adaptation and Mitigation Taxonomy

A controlled vocabulary for describing climate adaptation and mitigation in agricultural research. Each concept comes with its definition and source, the other words people use for it, its relations to other concepts, and the conditions under which it counts as adaptation or mitigation.

For example: Drought · Seed system · Index insurance · Agroforestry · Smallholder farmer
