CGIAR Climate Adaptation and Mitigation Taxonomy

739 concepts for describing climate adaptation and mitigation in agricultural research: definitions with their sources, 982 synonyms, 300 typed relations, and the conditions under which a term counts as adaptation or mitigation. Version 5.0.0-draft, 2026-09-23.
