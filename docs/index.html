CGIAR Climate Adaptation and Mitigation Taxonomy

A controlled vocabulary for describing climate adaptation and mitigation in agricultural research: 768 concepts with definitions and their sources, 971 synonyms, 351 typed relations, and the conditions under which a term counts as adaptation or mitigation. Version 6.0.0-draft.
