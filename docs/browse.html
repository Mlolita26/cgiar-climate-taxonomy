FacetWhat it holdsTerms
Climate riskPotential for adverse consequences of climate and its determinants and manifestations; the adaptation-side anchor. Prefix CRK_.71
Climate driverCausal-side mitigation anchor: greenhouse gases, emission sources, and the Climate change and Climate variability drivers. Prefix CDI_.18
SystemAgri-food and ecological systems and components acted upon or affected. Prefix SYS_.118
InterventionAn action or activity taken to adapt or mitigate. Prefix INT_.259
OutputA tangible product, service, or deliverable produced by an intervention. Prefix OUT_.111
OutcomeA neutral state or property that changes as a result; direction applied at tagging time. Prefix OTC_.69
ImpactA long-term or higher-order effect. Prefix IMP_.6
Adoption pathwayA modifier describing the stage or degree of uptake and scaling; never used alone. Prefix ADP_.5
Breeding traitA heritable characteristic targeted by breeding. Prefix TRT_.10
StakeholderAn actor or social group involved in or affected by climate action. Prefix STK_.70
Cross-cutting conceptA construct that qualifies other terms rather than occupying a position on the results chain: normative principles, named ways of working, and greenhouse-gas accounting constructs. Prefix CCC_. A term belongs here only if it cannot fill a pathway element on its own; if it can, it belongs in a chain facet.31
