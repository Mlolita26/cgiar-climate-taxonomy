NameComposite index
DefinitionQuantitative measure that aggregates multiple individual indicators into a single value, using a defined weighting and aggregation method, to summarise a complex climate-adaptation concept such as risk, vulnerability, or resilience.
Source: OECD/JRC Handbook on Constructing Composite Indicators (2008)
Synonymscomposite indicator
FacetOutput
Pathway stageOutput
BroaderTop concept of the facet
NarrowerAdaptive-capacity index, Climate resilience index, Climate vulnerability index
Climate areaAdaptation
Tagging roleContext-dependent: counts only when Output addresses a climate hazard, OR is produced by a qualifying intervention, OR a stand-alone adaptation cross-cutting concept applies to it, OR produces a qualifying outcome; NOT adaptation when it is a general product with no climate-risk framing and no link to a stand-alone adaptation term.
