NameAdoption
DefinitionFirst-time uptake of an adaptation product, practice, service, or technology by an intended user, distinct from sustained use thereafter.
Source: AGROVOC (adoption of innovations); Rogers diffusion of innovations
Synonymsuptake

Tagging roleContext-dependent: counts only when Adoption pathway is a modifier, never stand-alone; counts as adaptation when the intervention, output, or outcome being made available, accessed, adopted, used, or scaled is itself a stand-alone adaptation term or addresses a climate hazard; NOT adaptation when the thing adopted or scaled is a generic (non-adaptation) technology, product, or practice.
