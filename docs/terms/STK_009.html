NameSocial group
DefinitionIndividuals grouped by a shared social identity or position - such as gender, age, indigeneity, or mobility - that shapes their differential exposure, vulnerability, and adaptive capacity to climate hazards in agri-food systems.
Source: AGROVOC: social groups

Tagging roleContext-dependent: counts only when Stakeholder is engaged in a qualifying intervention (adopts, finances, or promotes), OR benefits from a qualifying outcome or impact; NOT adaptation when named only as an actor in a value chain or project, with no climate-risk role.
