Cattle

Livestock system based on raising cattle (Bos spp.) for milk, meat, draught, or assets.

Definition source: AGROVOC: cattle

Synonyms
bovine
Broader
Livestock system
Narrower
Dairy cattle, Beef cattle
Relations
emits Methane
Counts as adaptation
System is exposed to a climate hazard, OR is acted on by a hazard-addressing intervention, OR benefits from a qualifying outcome or an adaptation-specific impact; NOT adaptation when named only as the sector or commodity of work, with no exposure and no hazard-addressing action on it.
Example of use
Climate context: exposed to climate hazards affecting feed, water, heat stress, and animal health.
