NameAvoided land-use-change emissions
DefinitionAvoided emissions consisting of the greenhouse gas release prevented by retaining existing land cover and its carbon stock rather than converting it to another use.
Source: IPCC
Synonymsavoided land-use change, avoided deforestation emissions
