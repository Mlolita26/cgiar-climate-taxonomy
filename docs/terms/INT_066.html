Ecosystem restoration

NameEcosystem restoration
DefinitionLandscape management that assists the recovery of degraded, damaged, or destroyed ecosystems to restore their structure, function, and services.
Source: UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration
Synonymsecological restoration, landscape restoration
FacetIntervention
Pathway stageIntervention
BroaderLandscape management
NarrowerForest restoration, Land restoration, Mangrove restoration, Peatland restoration, Rangeland restoration, Salt marsh restoration, Seagrass restoration
RelationssequestersCarbon sink
producesCarbon sequestered
applies-toNatural ecosystem
Climate areaAdaptation + Mitigation
Tagging roleContext-dependent: counts only when Adaptation: Intervention addresses a climate hazard, OR produces a qualifying outcome (a stand-alone adaptation outcome, or an outcome measured against a climate hazard), OR produces a stand-alone adaptation impact; NOT adaptation when done purely for productivity, market, or development with no hazard link and no hazard-referenced result. Mitigation: Intervention reduces emissions of a source or gas, OR sequesters carbon, OR produces a mitigation outcome; NOT mitigation when done for productivity, market, or development reasons only, with no emissions link and no emissions-referenced result.
Review statusawaiting internal review Not part of the external validation. Reviewed internally, by mapping the term to CGIAR domains.
Example of useClimate context: including climate regulation and resilience.
