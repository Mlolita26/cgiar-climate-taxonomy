NameSeaweed
DefinitionAquatic system based on the cultivation of marine macroalgae for food, feed, or other products, providing blue-carbon and coastal benefits.
Source: FAO Fisheries and Aquaculture (seaweed culture)
Synonymsmacroalgae

Tagging roleContext-dependent: counts only when System is exposed to a climate hazard, OR is acted on by a hazard-addressing intervention, OR benefits from a qualifying outcome or a stand-alone adaptation impact; NOT adaptation when named only as the sector or commodity of work, with no exposure and no hazard-addressing action on it.
Review statusawaiting internal review Not part of the external validation. Reviewed internally, by mapping the term to CGIAR domains.
Example of useClimate context: exposed to climate hazards such as ocean warming and acidification.
