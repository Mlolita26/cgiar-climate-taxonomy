NameFood system
DefinitionSystem comprising the actors, activities, and interactions involved in the production, aggregation, processing, distribution, consumption, and disposal of food, together with the broader environments and outcomes they generate, whose components are exposed.
Source: FAO/HLPE Report 12 (2017) · reference
Synonymsagrifood system, Blue food system
FacetSystem
Pathway stageRationale
BroaderTop concept of the facet
NarrowerClimate-smart food system, Indigenous food system, Local food system, Urban and peri-urban food system
Climate areaAdaptation
Tagging roleContext-dependent: counts only when System is exposed to a climate hazard, OR is acted on by a hazard-addressing intervention, OR benefits from a qualifying outcome or a stand-alone adaptation impact; NOT adaptation when named only as the sector or commodity of work, with no exposure and no hazard-addressing action on it.
Review statusawaiting external validation Selected for validation by the climate community under the version 6 rules: every stand-alone term, and the context-dependent terms whose meaning the climate community sets (for example Rainfall) or that name a broad category of systems (for example Crop system).
Example of useCGIAR climate risk profiles assess value-chain vulnerabilities and adaptation opportunities across food systems. | Climate context: sensitive to climate hazards.
ProvenanceDomain expert; Lexicon consensus
