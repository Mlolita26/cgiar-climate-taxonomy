Biofertilizer

NameBiofertilizer
DefinitionFertilizer containing living micro-organisms that, when applied to seeds, plants, or soil, enhances nutrient availability or uptake, supporting productivity and soil function under climate stress.
Source: AGROVOC: biofertilizers
Synonymsbiological fertilizer, microbial inoculant

Tagging roleContext-dependent: counts only when Intervention addresses a climate hazard, OR produces a qualifying outcome (a stand-alone adaptation outcome, or an outcome measured against a climate hazard), OR produces a stand-alone adaptation impact; NOT adaptation when done purely for productivity, market, or development with no hazard link and no hazard-referenced result.
