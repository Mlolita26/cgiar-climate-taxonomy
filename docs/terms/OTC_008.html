Soil function

Outcome construct denoting the capacity of soil to sustain its biological, chemical, and physical processes - including nutrient cycling, water retention, and structure - treated neutrally as the result whose level may be maintained or degraded.

Definition source: FAO (soil functions); ITPS · reference

Synonyms
soil health, soil resilience
Broader
Top concept of the scheme
Narrower
none
Relations
reduces Climate sensitivity
Counts as adaptation
Outcome is measured against a climate hazard (e.g. yield under drought), OR is produced by a qualifying intervention, OR an adaptation-specific principle or cross-cutting concept applies to it; NOT adaptation when it is a plain level or quantity result not framed against a climate stressor.
Example of use
Climate-smart village work on soil and water management maintained soil function under drought conditions. | Climate context: the level may change under drought, heat, or erratic rainfall.
