NameWater-energy-food nexus
DefinitionInterdependencies between water, energy, and food systems, used to analyse trade-offs and synergies when planning interventions that affect more than one of the three sectors.
Source: FAO Water-Energy-Food Nexus
SynonymsWEF nexus, WEFE nexus, water-energy-food-ecosystem nexus, water, energy and food nexus
