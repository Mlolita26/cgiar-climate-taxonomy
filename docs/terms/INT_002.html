Breeding

Intervention that develops improved crop varieties, livestock breeds, or aquatic strains through directed selection and recombination.

Definition source: AGROVOC: plant breeding

Synonyms
genetic improvement, varietal development

Counts as adaptation
Intervention addresses a climate hazard, OR produces a qualifying outcome (adaptation-specific or hazard-conditioned), OR produces an adaptation-specific impact; NOT adaptation when done purely for productivity, market, or development with no hazard link and no hazard-referenced result.
Example of use
Climate context: including for traits conferring tolerance to climate stresses such as drought, heat, or flooding.
