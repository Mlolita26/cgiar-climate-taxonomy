NameAgropastoralist
DefinitionPastoralist whose household combines crop farming with livestock keeping, using both as complementary risk-management strategies under climate stress.
Source: AGROVOC: agropastoral systems
Synonymsmixed crop-livestock households, farmer-herders

Tagging roleContext-dependent: counts only when Stakeholder is engaged in a qualifying intervention (adopts, finances, or promotes), OR benefits from a qualifying outcome or impact; NOT adaptation when named only as an actor in a value chain or project, with no climate-risk role.
Review statusawaiting internal review Not part of the external validation. Reviewed internally, by mapping the term to CGIAR domains.
