# Changelog

## 5.0.0-draft (2026-09-23)
First publication as a FAIR vocabulary (SKOS). Content is version 5 of the taxonomy, built 2026-09-04. Draft status.

## Version 5 compared with version 4

- ADDED: 128
- DEFINITION CHANGED: 219
- DESCRIPTOR CHANGED: 95
- FACET CHANGED: 4
- LEVEL CHANGED: 174
- RE-PARENTED: 99
- RENAMED: 27
- RETIRED: 15
- SOURCE CHANGED: 21
Synonyms: 219 added, 168 removed, 13 moved to another concept.
