Anyone in CGIAR, and partners, can propose to add, change or retire a term. A proposal is a GitHub issue with a short form. If you do not have a GitHub account, ask one of the custodians to open the issue for you.

<>

What to include

  • One concept per request, with its preferred label in sentence case.
  • A definition that opens with the broader concept, and its source: IPCC, AGROVOC, FAO, WMO, UNFCCC or a cited publication.
  • Evidence that the term is used in CGIAR work: a PRMS result, a plan or a report.
  • Your ORCID, if you have one, so that the contribution is credited in the release notes.

How a proposal is decided

The custodian screens each proposal with five questions, in this order:

  1. Is it already here? If the concept exists under another word, the proposal becomes a synonym.
  2. Is it one concept? A phrase that only adds an adjective to an existing term is not a new concept.
  3. Does it have a defensible definition, from a named source?
  4. Is it used in CGIAR reporting or plans?
  5. Where does it sit? One facet, one broader term, and the broader term must be a true "is a" parent.

The custodian answers in the issue with the reason and records the decision in governance/decisions.csv. Accepted changes appear in the next release. Refused proposals stay visible, so that the same question is not reopened without new evidence. Full rules: GOVERNANCE.md.

Rules that never change

  • An identifier, once published, is never reused for another concept.
  • An identifier does not change when its term moves, also when it moves to another facet.
  • A retired term is not deleted: it stays, marked deprecated, and points to its successor.
