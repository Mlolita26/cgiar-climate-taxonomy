The <> is a controlled vocabulary for describing climate adaptation and mitigation in agricultural research results. It gives each concept a definition with its source, the other words people use for it, its place in a hierarchy, typed relations to other concepts, and the conditions under which it counts as adaptation or mitigation. It was developed by the CGIAR MELIAF Climate Adaptation Activator and is used to tag CGIAR results reported in PRMS.

Scope

<> concepts in <> facets, from climate risks and drivers to interventions, outputs, outcomes, impacts and stakeholders. The browser lists the facets with their definitions. The vocabulary is published in SKOS, the web standard for thesauri and taxonomies, with a small documented extension for the typed relations and the tagging rules.

Who maintains it

Custodian: <>. The custodian decides what enters, changes or leaves the vocabulary and publishes the releases. Anyone can propose a change: see Contribute. The rules are in GOVERNANCE.md.

How to cite

<>

Machine-readable citation: CITATION.cff. A permanent DOI will be issued through Zenodo with the first release after the licence is confirmed.

Licence

Proposed: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), pending confirmation by the custodian. Many definitions cite published sources (IPCC, AGROVOC, FAO, WMO, UNFCCC); attribution keeps that chain of credit visible. Details: LICENSE.md.

Identifiers and addresses

Every concept has an identifier such as CRK_102 that is never reused, and does not change when the term moves. A retired term keeps its identifier and its page, marked deprecated and pointing to its successor; <> identifiers are kept this way. Today the addresses use the GitHub Pages site (<>terms/ID); the planned permanent form is <>ID.
